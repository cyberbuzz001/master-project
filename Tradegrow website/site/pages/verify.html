Independent verification

How to verify our operating details independently

We encourage every trader and investor to verify platform credentials and intermediary connections directly on official regulatory registries.

  1. Check Market Intermediary Registration on SEBI

    Trade Grow operates as a technology and trading platform brand. Securities transactions are facilitated through relevant registered intermediaries. You can verify intermediary certifications and stock broker records directly in the SEBI intermediaries database.

    Verify on SEBI Intermediaries Search

  2. Check Exchange Membership on NSE & BSE

    Search the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) member directories to verify registered trading members, approved market segments, and clearing relationships.

    Verify on NSE Member Search Verify on BSE Member Directory

  3. Check Depository Participant & Custody Safeguards

    Client shares and securities sit in demat accounts with CDSL or NSDL under depository regulations. Client funds and securities are handled according to the applicable intermediary structure and regulatory requirements.

    CDSL DP Directory NSDL DP Search

  4. Web App — Add shortcut to home screen

    Trade Grow is built as a fast, secure Web Application running directly in your browser without requiring store downloads. For a native app experience, create a shortcut to your home screen: on iOS Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android Chrome tap Menu (three dots) → Install app / Add to Home screen.

  5. Verify Official Support Desk & Communication

    Always confirm contact phone numbers and emails against the official published channels on this trust center (+91 95896 15649 and info@tradegrowx.in). Never communicate with unverified numbers claiming to represent Trade Grow.

  6. Dedicated Compliance Cell & Resolution Support

    If you have any questions regarding platform governance, entity structure, or intermediary routing, reach our Compliance Officer directly at info@tradegrowx.in. All queries receive formal evaluation within 24-48 hours.
