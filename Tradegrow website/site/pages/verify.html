Independent verification

How to verify us yourself, in about five minutes

These steps work for any Indian broker, not just us. We would genuinely encourage you to run them on every platform you are considering.

  1. Check the SEBI registration number

    Open the SEBI intermediaries search and look up the registration number published above. Confirm the entity name on SEBI’s page matches the legal entity name we have published — not just the brand name. Brand names are not registered; entities are.

    Open SEBI intermediaries search

  2. Check exchange membership

    Search the NSE and BSE member directories for the member code above. The exchange listing should show the same legal entity and the segments we claim to operate in.

    NSE member search BSE member directory

  3. Check the depository participant listing

    Your shares sit in a demat account with CDSL or NSDL, not with the broker. Look up the DP ID on the depository’s own site and confirm it belongs to the same entity.

    CDSL DP list NSDL DP search

  4. Check the company on the MCA portal

    Search the CIN on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs master data page. You will see the incorporation date, registered office and company status. A company incorporated last month is not disqualifying — but you should know it.

    MCA company master data

  5. Check the app listing matches the entity

    On Google Play or the App Store, look at the developer name on the listing — not the app name. It should match the registered legal entity. A mismatch here is a serious warning sign.

  6. Call the number published on the exchange page, not the one in a message

    If someone contacted you claiming to be from Trade Grow, confirm the number against the official contact published here and on the exchange member listing. Fraud almost always starts with a number you were given rather than a number you looked up.

  7. Ask us anything that is still unclear

    If a detail on this page is blank and you need it before deciding, ask our Compliance Officer directly. “We will confirm and revert” is an acceptable answer. “Don’t worry about that” is not.
