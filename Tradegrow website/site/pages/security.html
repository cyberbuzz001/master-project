Security

What we actually do, and what we don’t claim

Financial websites tend to describe security with adjectives — bank-grade, military-grade, fully secure. Those words are unregulated and mean nothing in particular. This page lists specific controls instead, and marks the ones not yet live as not yet live.

We do not claim that Trade Grow is completely safe from every risk. No platform holding money and market access can honestly say that. What we can tell you is which controls exist, which are still being built, and what part of your account’s safety depends on you rather than on us.

The part that depends on you

Most account compromises in Indian broking do not involve breaking into the broker. They involve persuading the customer to hand over a credential. That makes these the highest- value controls in the entire system, and they are yours.

  • 1
    Never share an OTP, password or PIN with anyone

    Including anyone claiming to be from Trade Grow, your bank, or a regulator.

  • 2
    Never install a screen-sharing or remote-access app on request

    There is no support scenario at Trade Grow that requires it.

  • 3
    Use a password you do not use anywhere else

    Credential reuse is how a breach at an unrelated website becomes a problem for your trading account.

  • 4
    Keep your registered mobile and email current

    Alerts are only useful if they reach you. Update them if you change numbers.

  • 5
    Read your contract notes and ledger

    An unfamiliar trade or debit is far easier to resolve in days than in months.

  • 6
    Download the app only from the official store listing

    Check the developer name matches our registered entity. Never install an APK sent in a message.
