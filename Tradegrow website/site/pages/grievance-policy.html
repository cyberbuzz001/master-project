1. Resolution Philosophy

At Trade Grow, an inquiry or problem is categorized systematically:

  • Service Query: Clarifications regarding charges, app features, or onboarding guidance.
  • Formal Grievance: Any formal expression of dissatisfaction regarding unauthorized trades, disputed charges, delayed payouts, or compliance breaches.

All formal grievances are assigned a unique tracking number, logged into our central audit system, and monitored against SEBI-mandated resolution deadlines.

3. External Avenues: SEBI SCORES & SMART ODR

If you are dissatisfied with our internal resolution, you do not need our permission to escalate externally. The following statutory mechanisms are independently available:

SEBI SCORES Portal (SEBI Complaints Redress System)

SEBI provides an online grievance filing platform. You can lodge complaints directly against any registered intermediary:

Visit SEBI SCORES (scores.sebi.gov.in) →

Toll-Free SEBI Investor Helpline: 1800 22 7575 / 1800 266 7575

SMART ODR Portal (Securities Market Approach for Resolution Through ODR)

SEBI has established an online dispute resolution mechanism facilitating independent conciliation and arbitration for securities market disputes:

Visit SMART ODR Portal (smartodr.in) →

4. Regulatory Turnaround Times (TAT)

  • Acknowledgment: Within 24 hours of ticket receipt.
  • Internal Resolution (L1 / L2): Within 7 to 15 calendar days.
  • Compliance Desk (L3): Maximum 30 calendar days as per SEBI regulations.
  • Exchange / SCORES Escalation: Managed under relevant regulatory timeframes.
