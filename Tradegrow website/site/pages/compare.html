What a comparison table cannot tell you

Price is the easiest thing to tabulate and often not the most important thing. Things that do not fit in a table but matter more than a few rupees of brokerage:

  • Whether the platform stays up on a high-volume day, when you most need to exit
  • How long it takes to reach a human when something has gone wrong with your money
  • Whether withdrawal requests are processed on the stated timeline, consistently
  • Whether the broker’s charge list matches the contract notes it actually issues

We are new, so we have no track record on any of these. We are not going to pretend otherwise, and you should weigh that honestly against whatever pricing advantage exists.
