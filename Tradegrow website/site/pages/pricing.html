Why we will not print “zero tax”

Our marketing team, like every broker’s marketing team, would prefer a shorter headline. But taxes on securities transactions in India are set by law. A broker cannot waive STT, GST or stamp duty any more than a shop can waive GST on a shirt.

If a brokerage plan genuinely offers nil brokerage, that is a real and worthwhile thing and we will say so — with the conditions attached. What we will not do is let the word “zero” quietly expand to cover charges we do not control.

The practical consequence for you: when comparing brokers, compare the total cost of a trade, not the headline. The statutory part is identical everywhere.

Where to check what you were charged

After every trading day on which you transact, you receive a contract note. It itemises brokerage and each statutory charge as a separate line. That document, not this page and not the calculator, is the authoritative record.

If a charge appears on your contract note that is not in our published Schedule of Charges, please raise it through support. We will treat it as a grievance with a defined response time, not as a general query.
