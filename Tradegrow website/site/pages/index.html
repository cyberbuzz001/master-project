Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers regarding charges, account opening, regulatory status, and security.

How does Trade Grow charge for trades? ▾
Trade Grow charges a flat brokerage of ₹20 or 0.05% (whichever is lower) per executed order. In addition to our brokerage, all trades in Indian markets attract statutory charges levied by the Government, Exchanges, and SEBI (STT, Stamp Duty, GST, Exchange Turnover Fees, and SEBI charges). We itemize every single charge on your contract note with zero markup.
How can I verify Trade Grow's regulatory registrations? ▾
You can independently verify Trade Grow by visiting our Verify page, which provides direct links to the official SEBI directory, MCA corporate registry, and exchange member lists. We publish actual lookup codes rather than static image certificates.
What documents are required to open an account? ▾
You will need your PAN card, Aadhaar card (linked to an active mobile number for OTP verification), proof of bank account (cancelled cheque or bank statement), and a selfie for In-Person Verification (IPV). For F&O trading, 6 months of bank statement or salary slip is required as income proof.
Does Trade Grow offer stock tips or guaranteed returns? ▾
No. Trade Grow is strictly a trading technology and execution platform. We do not offer stock tips, trade calls, portfolio management, or guaranteed returns. Any person claiming to offer assured returns in the name of Trade Grow is fraudulent and should be reported to our compliance officer.
How are my funds and securities protected? ▾
Your securities are held directly in your personal Demat account with the central depository (CDSL/NSDL), not on Trade Grow's balance sheet. Client funds are segregated in designated settlement accounts with clearing corporations according to strict SEBI regulations.
